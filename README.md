## ✦ About Me

I am a software engineer and researcher who builds **end-to-end products, autonomous AI systems, and security tooling**.

Most of what I build starts the same way: something in the real world is slow, opaque, or unreliable, and I want to see if I can design and ship a working fix from scratch. Over the past year, that instinct has taken me across wildly different domains: from **regulatory document forensics** and **deterministic B2B web-research agents** to **offline on-device Android edge-AI**, **Aditya-L1 solar flare X-ray forecasting**, **Chandrayaan-2 lunar radar analysis**, and **urban flood early-warning systems**.

I treat AI as a core engineering multiplier, never a black-box crutch. My focus is on **evidence-first AI**: pairing LLMs and machine learning with deterministic verification, physical laws, cryptographic provenance, and strict data-leakage guards so systems never guess when they should verify.

---

## ✦ How I Build

- **Problem First, Stack Second:** Technology stacks are tools, not identities. I start by understanding *why* a problem matters to the user, what constraints actually govern the domain, and what makes a solution measurably better than the status quo.
- **Grounded AI & Zero Tolerance for Bluffing:** An AI model is only as useful as the verification layer around it. Whether building tool-calling copilots, web agents, or multi-horizon classifiers, I enforce deterministic contracts, provenance tracking, and leakage tripwires so hallucinations and degenerate metrics get caught early.
- **End-to-End Ownership:** I like operating as a one-person product team: scoping the problem, researching unfamiliar domain math or protocols in days, designing the architecture, and shipping a live, testable system.
- **Break Things to Understand How They Fail:** My background in offensive cybersecurity, CTFs, and Linux internals shapes how I write software. Understanding how systems break is the fastest way to spot subtle design flaws before they reach production.
