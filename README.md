## ✦ About Me

I'm a builder exploring the intersection of intelligence, software, and hardware. I mostly focus on **Applied AI & Autonomous Agents, Full-Stack Product Engineering, Linux, and Cybersecurity**.

I love finding real problems, framing what actually matters, and driving AI to build and ship complete systems from scratch. I also enjoy breaking things to understand how they fail, which is the best way to tell when a design or a model is bluffing. Long-term, I'm focused on **intelligent aerospace software and autonomous systems** that will push humanity into deep space.

## ✦ Interests

1. **Autonomous AI & Agents** - Directing LLMs and grounded models to research unfamiliar domains, reason over real constraints, and automate complex workflows.
2. **Space & Planetary AI** - Applying machine learning to solar astrophysics, satellite earth observation, and lunar mission planning.
3. **Product Building** - Owning problems end-to-end and shipping live solutions from a blank page.
4. **Cybersecurity & CTFs** - Understanding vulnerabilities and adversarial failure modes to build resilient systems.
5. **Edge AI & IoT** - Running multimodal perception and physics-informed ML on real-world sensors and devices.
6. **Systems & Linux** - Mastering core computer science fundamentals beneath the abstractions.
